Positions & coverage: community ADS-B (adsb.lol, ODbL — this open repository republishes the derived flight database, satisfying share-alike). Aircraft class from the NZ CAA register. Aerodromes/runways: OurAirports. Sampled every ~5 min from first light to last light NZ time. GitHub's scheduler throttles 5-minutely crons to roughly hourly, so sampling is driven from inside an hourly job that loops, rather than by the schedule itself. Direct km is aerodrome to aerodrome and is what the range slider tests; Flown km is the length of the track actually flown. A trip is archived only once it has been seen moving between two different aerodromes, so its distance is measured rather than guessed — traces still airborne, or seen only once, are dropped. Hops that begin and end between two sweeps can still be missed, so leg counts stay lower bounds. Coverage is thin at low level in Fiordland / Stewart Island / West Coast. The Live tab shows the last committed sweep, not the current sky — browsers cannot query adsb.lol directly.
